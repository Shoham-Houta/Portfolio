The Greenholt Phish — Email & Attachment Triage

  • Traced the Received chain to the originating host and found the message came from an IP the sender domain's own SPF record does not authorise.
  • Caught a Reply-To pointing at a free mailbox while the From claimed a real corporate domain — replies would have gone straight to attacker-controlled infrastructure.
  • Identified the attached "PDF" as a RAR archive disguised by its extension. Triaged by hash and metadata only; the attachment was never extracted or executed.
  • Verdict: malicious — phishing carrying a malware payload under a spoofed corporate identity.
